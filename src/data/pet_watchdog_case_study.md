# PetWatchdog – Case Study

## Overview
PetWatchdog is a UK-based pet review platform developed as part of Straja Consultancy. The platform enables users to review, rate, and discover pet-related services, supporting better decision-making for pet owners.

This project focused on full-stack architecture design, performance optimization, and UX improvements with a strong emphasis on SEO and conversion rate optimization.

---

## Business Context
The pet services market in the UK is highly fragmented, with limited centralized platforms for transparent service reviews. PetWatchdog aimed to solve:

- Lack of trust and transparency in pet services
- Poor discoverability of quality providers
- Inefficient user onboarding flows

**Goal:** Build a scalable review platform with high SEO visibility and strong user acquisition metrics.
